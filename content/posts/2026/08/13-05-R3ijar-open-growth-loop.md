## Action Summary

Open Growth Loop is a technical documentation assistant designed to help maintainers of open-source repositories prioritize their work. It analyzes the repository's hygiene, assesses its readiness for contributors, and suggests actionable next steps based on evidence. The action supports both local-only mode and integration with GitHub APIs to gather additional context.

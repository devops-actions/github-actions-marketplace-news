## Action Summary

Regula is an AI governance scanning tool that checks local source-code folders for potential risks related to AI regulations such as the EU AI Act, South Korea's AI Basic Act, and Colorado SB 26-189. It provides a detailed review trail and candidate risk categories, allowing developers or governance leads to decide what further investigation is needed. The tool does not determine legal classification or compliance but focuses on identifying patterns that may need human review.

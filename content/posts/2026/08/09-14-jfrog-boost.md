## Action Summary

Boost is an OpenTelemetry-based tool that reduces noise in log output by trimming safe data while preserving essential information such as errors, timings, and cache hits. It optimizes agent performance without affecting their output quality, ensuring agents remain sharp and efficient. Boost supports various platforms, including macOS, Linux, Windows WSL, and Windows PowerShell, and is available for installation via curl or PowerShell scripts.

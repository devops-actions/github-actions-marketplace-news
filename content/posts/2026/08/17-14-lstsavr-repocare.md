## Action Summary

RepoCare is a local tool that checks the health and completeness of open-source projects, providing immediate feedback on common maintenance gaps such as documentation, license files, and security policies. It helps maintainers identify areas for improvement by generating actionable reports without uploading source code or requiring API keys. RepoCare is designed to be fast and efficient, ensuring that developers can quickly understand where their repositories fall short in terms of quality and completeness.

## Action Summary

GH Stars is an open-source solution for tracking GitHub stars in self-hosted environments. It automates the process of backfilling stargazer timestamps and maintaining daily star counts to ensure accuracy even after stars are removed. The action uses the `GITHUB_TOKEN` to fetch recent stargazers and generate a chart that can be embedded in repository READMEs using Markdown.

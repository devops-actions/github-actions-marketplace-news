## Action Summary

**Crosspost your latest content from GitHub Actions to multiple social networks using a sitemap or RSS/Atom feed.** This action filters and posts content by age and URL patterns, with options for dry-run mode, failure strategies, and metadata prefilling. The user needs to configure the `feed-url` and select the desired social networks for posting.

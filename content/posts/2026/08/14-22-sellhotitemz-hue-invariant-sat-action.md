## Action Summary

Invariant SAT ZK Circuit Security Scanner automates zero-knowledge circuit soundness and exploit witness scanning for GitHub CI/CD pipelines. It identifies under-constrained signals and proof forgery vulnerabilities before deploying code to production, ensuring the security of cryptographic circuits used in blockchain applications. The action supports various types of circuits including R1CS, SP1 zkVM AIR traces, Halo2 Plonkish matrices, and Aztec Noir smart contracts.

## Action Summary

The Sync Action automates the synchronization of base files, configurations, and scripts across the [SDK-Fabric](https://sdk-fabric.org/) ecosystem. It enables SDK repositories to dynamically fetch templates from their respective central template repositories (e.g., `sdk-fabric/sdk-template-csharp`, `sdk-fabric/sdk-template-python`) and apply them automatically, streamlining the development process and reducing redundancy.

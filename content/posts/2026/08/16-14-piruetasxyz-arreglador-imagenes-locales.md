## Action Summary

`arreglador-imagenes-locales` is a GitHub Action that detects images in markdown files that are not local to the repository, such as external URLs or uploaded images through drag-and-drop. Its purpose is to ensure offline accessibility by reporting and marking any non-local images so they can be uploaded to the repo and referenced with relative paths. The action does not download or fix anything but simply checks for and reports missing files.

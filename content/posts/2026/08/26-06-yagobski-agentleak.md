## Action Summary

AgentLeak is a privacy-leakage testing tool designed to audit AI agents across various execution channels. It ensures that sensitive data is not leaked through internal communication and logs by scoring the agent's performance with the AgentRisk metric, which measures the severity of potential privacy breaches. The Action automates this process within continuous integration pipelines.

## Action Summary

This GitHub Action automates the packaging and uploading of Factorio mods to the Mod Portal. It simplifies the workflow by handling dependency management, mod validation, and automated upload processes. The action provides features for both validating and compressing the mod and uploading it to the portal, with options to skip updating mod details if necessary.

## Action Summary

This GitHub Action automates the linking of discovered services from the Postman Insights DaemonSet agent to a specific workspace and environment in your API Catalog. It solves the problem of manual linking by providing a streamlined process that automatically connects new services as they are deployed, ensuring all relevant information is added directly to your catalog.

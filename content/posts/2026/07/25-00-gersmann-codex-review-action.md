## Action Summary

The Codex Review Action automates the process of reviewing and editing pull requests using OpenAI's GPT models. It posts inline review comments and a PR-level summary, and can apply focused edits on demand when trusted users comment `/codex`. The action supports both review and act modes, allowing for minimal or detailed edits based on the specified reasoning effort.

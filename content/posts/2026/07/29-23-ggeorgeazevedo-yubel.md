Yubel is an all-seeing dynamic security orchestrator that automates the scanning of web apps, REST/GraphQL APIs, cloud assets, containers, and Kubernetes using a variety of open-source DAST engines. It correlates findings across different engines to uncover multi-step attack chains and provides comprehensive reports in JSON, HTML dashboard, Markdown, and SARIF formats. Yubel runs locally, is air-gapped by design, and supports CI/CD pipelines, making it suitable for environments with restricted access and compliance requirements.

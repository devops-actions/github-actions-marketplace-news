## Action Summary

AI Harness Doctor is a GitHub Action that automates the review of AI harness files (e.g., `AGENTS.md`, `CLAUDE.md`) to ensure they are up-to-date, consistent, and effective. It helps in consolidating scattered guidance into one human-owned `AGENTS.md` file, keeping tool-specific files as small pointers, and measuring whether the resulting harness improves agent answers. The action is useful for maintaining a healthy AI-driven development environment by identifying inconsistencies and optimizing documentation.

## Action Summary

PixLog is a Git-compatible tool that provides visual diffs and provenance for images in Git repositories. It helps developers understand which pixels have changed and who made them changes, while also providing policy checks to ensure consistency and prevent accidental or malicious modifications. PixLog includes features like visual blame to trace image regions back to their origin commits, AI provenance tracking for AI-generated assets, and a visual history feature that shows pixel metrics and r changes between revisions.

## Action Summary

The action automates AI-driven security audits for various tools such as Claude Code, Gemini CLI, Cursor, and GitHub Copilot. It uses a multi-agent approach to review findings across the entire repository or diff/PR scope, ensuring robustness and accuracy by having independent eyes verify each finding skeptically and adversarially. The key capabilities include profiling the repo, designing an investigation plan based on its architecture, and applying findings to the codebase.
